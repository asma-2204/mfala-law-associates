Family Law Services

Family disputes often involve sensitive personal, financial and emotional issues that require careful legal handling. Muhammad Faheem Anjum Law Associates provides professional legal services in matters relating to divorce, khula, child custody, guardianship, maintenance and family appeals. Our legal team assists clients in protecting their legal rights while ensuring compliance with the Family Courts Act and other applicable laws of Pakistan. We aim to provide practical legal solutions through negotiation, mediation and court proceedings where necessary.
