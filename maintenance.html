Maintenance

Overview

Maintenance is one of the most frequently litigated matters before Family Courts in Pakistan. Financial support is a legal obligation that may arise between spouses, children and other dependents under applicable family laws. Disputes commonly occur when a husband fails to provide adequate financial support to his wife or children, or when maintenance payments are irregular, insufficient or completely discontinued.

Family Courts have the authority to determine and award maintenance according to the financial circumstances of the parties and the needs of the dependents. These proceedings often involve examination of income, expenses, living standards, educational costs and other relevant factors. Proper legal representation is essential to ensure that the rights of the claimant are fully protected and that all relevant evidence is presented before the court.

Maintenance disputes may arise during marriage, after separation, during divorce proceedings or even after dissolution of marriage in matters concerning children. Delays in obtaining financial support can create significant hardship for dependents and may affect education, healthcare and daily living expenses. Timely legal action is often necessary to secure lawful financial assistance and prevent further difficulties.
