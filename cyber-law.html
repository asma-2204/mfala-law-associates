Cyber Law

Overview

Cyber crimes have become increasingly common in Pakistan due to the rapid growth of digital communication, social media platforms, online banking and e-commerce activities. Individuals and businesses frequently face issues such as online fraud, identity theft, cyber harassment, unauthorized access to digital accounts, online defamation, financial scams and misuse of personal information. These offences can cause significant financial, personal and reputational harm.

The Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) and other applicable laws provide legal remedies against cyber offences and electronic crimes. However, cyber-related disputes often involve technical evidence and specialized legal procedures that require professional legal guidance and representation.

Muhammad Faheem Anjum Law Associates provides professional legal services relating to Cyber Law and digital offences. Our legal team assists clients in reporting cyber crimes, pursuing legal remedies, defending allegations and representing individuals and businesses before the relevant authorities and courts.

Whether the matter involves cyber harassment, online fraud, identity theft, data misuse, digital defamation or other technology-related disputes, our firm remains committed to protecting our clients' rights through effective legal representation.
