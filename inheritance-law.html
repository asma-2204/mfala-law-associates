Inheritance Law

Overview

Inheritance disputes are among the most sensitive and complex legal matters in Pakistan. Family members frequently face disagreements regarding the distribution of inherited property, ownership rights, ancestral assets, agricultural land, residential properties, commercial properties and other movable or immovable assets left behind by a deceased person.

Such disputes often arise due to misunderstandings regarding legal shares, concealment of assets, illegal occupation of inherited property or refusal by certain heirs to recognize the lawful rights of other family members.

Under Pakistani law, legal heirs are entitled to receive their respective inheritance shares according to the applicable principles of succession and inheritance. However, many individuals, particularly women, widows and minor children, are unlawfully deprived of their rightful shares due to family pressure, fraudulent transfers, forged documents or illegal possession by other parties.

These situations often result in prolonged disputes that require proper legal intervention and court proceedings.
