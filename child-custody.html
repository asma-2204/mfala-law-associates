Child Custody

Overview

Child Custody matters are among the most sensitive and important family disputes handled by Family Courts in Pakistan. Following divorce, separation or marital disputes, disagreements often arise regarding the care, upbringing, education and welfare of minor children. These matters require careful legal handling because the future, safety and well-being of the child remain the primary concern of the court.

Under Pakistani family laws, custody disputes are decided on the principle of welfare of the minor. Family Courts carefully examine the circumstances of each case, including the child's age, educational needs, living conditions, financial stability and overall welfare before making any decision. Every custody matter is unique and requires proper legal guidance to protect both parental rights and the interests of the child.

Many parents face difficulties in securing custody, visitation rights or protection against unlawful interference in the upbringing of their children. Disputes may also arise regarding relocation of children, educational decisions, healthcare matters and access rights of parents. Such situations often require immediate legal action to safeguard the interests of the child and ensure compliance with court orders.
