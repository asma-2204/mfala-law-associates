Child Custody in Pakistan: Welfare of the Minor and Visitation Rights

Child-custody disputes are among the most sensitive family cases because the court must consider the welfare and interests of the child alongside the legal positions of the parents.

The welfare of the child

Custody disputes are not decided simply by which parent demands custody more strongly. The court considers the circumstances of the minor and the evidence relevant to the child's welfare, upbringing, education, health, safety and overall interests.

Custody and visitation are different

Physical custody and visitation or access are related but distinct issues. A parent who does not have day-to-day custody may still seek appropriate contact with the child, subject to the court's assessment and any applicable orders.

Guardianship framework

The Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 is an important part of the legal framework for guardianship and custody-related proceedings. The facts of the case determine which provisions and procedural steps are relevant.

Evidence and practical preparation

School records, medical information, living arrangements, caregiving history, communication records and other evidence may be relevant. Parents should avoid coaching children, making threats or using the child as a messenger in the dispute.

Key takeaway

A custody case should remain focused on the child's welfare. Legal advice should be based on the child's circumstances, the parents' conduct and the evidence available.
